Welcome to the **YouTube AI Dream Team** — a complete system of 30 AI specialist agents, each with a detailed Standard Operating Procedure (SOP), ready-to-use prompt templates, and full API/tool setup guides.

This kit is designed for YouTube creators and business owners who want to run a professional, high-output YouTube channel **without hiring a full production team**.

---

## How to Use This Kit

**Step 1:** Start with `youtube_30_agents_roster.md` to understand the full system and how agents connect.

**Step 2:** Identify which department is your biggest bottleneck right now:
- Struggling with ideas? → Start with Department A (Agents 01–06)
- Struggling with content quality? → Start with Department B (Agents 07–11)
- Struggling with growth? → Start with Department F (Agents 26–30)

**Step 3:** Open the relevant agent SOP file and follow the Phase 1–3 workflow.

**Step 4:** Copy the prompt templates directly into Claude, ChatGPT, or your preferred AI tool.
