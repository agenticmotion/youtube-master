# The YouTube AI Dream Team: 30 Specialist Agents

## Department A: Channel Strategy & Ideation (Agents 1-6)

**Agent 01 — YouTube Channel Architect**
- Role: Orchestrates the overall channel strategy, defines the niche, and coordinates the other 29 agents.
- Tools: Notion, Google Sheets
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 02, 03, 04, 05, 06

**Agent 02 — Niche & Competitor Analyst**
- Role: Analyzes top competitors in the niche to identify content gaps and successful formats.
- Tools: Google Sheets, Apify
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic, YouTube Data API, Apify
- Collaborates with: Agents 01, 03, 04

**Agent 03 — Viral Topic Ideation Engine**
- Role: Generates high-potential video ideas based on search trends, competitor gaps, and audience interest.
- Tools: Google Sheets, Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic, YouTube Data API, Google Trends API
- Collaborates with: Agents 01, 02, 04, 05

**Agent 04 — Keyword & SEO Strategist**
- Role: Identifies high-volume, low-competition keywords for video titles, descriptions, and tags.
- Tools: Google Sheets, TubeBuddy/vidIQ (via API)
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic, YouTube Data API, SerpAPI
- Collaborates with: Agents 03, 05, 12

**Agent 05 — Title & Thumbnail Concept Generator**
- Role: Develops high-CTR title variations and thumbnail concepts before the video is even scripted.
- Tools: Notion, Google Sheets
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 03, 04, 06, 13

**Agent 06 — Audience Avatar Builder**
- Role: Creates and maintains a detailed profile of the target viewer to ensure content resonates.
- Tools: Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 01, 03, 07

## Department B: Pre-Production & Scripting (Agents 7-11)

**Agent 07 — Hook & Intro Writer**
- Role: Crafts the crucial first 30 seconds of the script to maximize viewer retention.
- Tools: Google Docs, Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 05, 06, 08

**Agent 08 — Core Script & Storyboard Writer**
- Role: Writes the main body of the video script, incorporating storytelling frameworks (e.g., Hero's Journey).
- Tools: Google Docs, Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 07, 09, 10

**Agent 09 — Retention & Pacing Optimizer**
- Role: Reviews scripts to ensure proper pacing, pattern interrupts, and visual cues to keep viewers engaged.
- Tools: Google Docs
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 08, 10, 14

**Agent 10 — Call-to-Action (CTA) Strategist**
- Role: Designs effective CTAs for subscriptions, likes, or lead magnets without disrupting the viewing experience.
- Tools: Google Docs
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 08, 09, 26

**Agent 11 — Shot List & B-Roll Planner**
- Role: Generates a detailed shot list and B-roll suggestions based on the finalized script.
- Tools: Google Sheets, Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 08, 09, 14

## Department C: Production & Post-Production (Agents 12-16)

**Agent 12 — Metadata & Description Writer**

- Tools: Google Docs, YouTube Studio
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic, YouTube Data API
- Collaborates with: Agents 04, 08, 17

**Agent 13 — Thumbnail Design Director**

- Collaborates with: Agents 05, 17

**Agent 14 — Video Editing Director**
- Role: Provides an editing brief, including pacing notes, sound effect suggestions, and graphic overlays.
- Tools: Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 09, 11, 15

**Agent 15 — Audio & Sound Design Consultant**
- Role: Suggests background music moods, sound effects, and audio mixing notes for the edit.
- Tools: Notion
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic
- Collaborates with: Agents 14

**Agent 16 — Subtitle & Caption Optimizer**
- Role: Generates and formats accurate, engaging subtitles (SRT/VTT) for the video.
- Tools: AssemblyAI, Google Docs
- APIs: OpenAI/Anthropic, AssemblyAI
- Collaborates with: Agents 08, 14
